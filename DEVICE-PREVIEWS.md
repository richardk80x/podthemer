# Device illustrations — 0.3.1

Original SwiftUI vector artwork lives in `DevicePreview` in `PodThemer.swift`. No product photographs are bundled. Reference: [Apple: Identify your iPod model](https://support.apple.com/en-us/103823), accessed 2026-10-31, including the front photos of Scroll/Touch Wheel, Dock Connector, Click Wheel and Mini models.

- Gen 1/2: white acrylic front, narrow polished edge, separate outer button ring, four diagonal button seams, recessed white scroll/touch wheel and center button. These generations share firmware and their front control arrangement; the preview does not claim to detect the mechanical wheel.
- Gen 3: rounded white casing, four circular red-orange illuminated touch controls in the correct order above the unlabelled touch wheel.
- Gen 4 monochrome: rounded white front, gray click wheel with integrated controls, white center button.
- Mini 1/2: narrower silver anodized aluminum case with shaded curved side edges, smaller correctly proportioned LCD, white click wheel. Both generations are shown in their silver finish. Firmware cannot identify finish or original capacity, so those are not inferred.

The LCD is generated by the existing firmware preview engine and composited independently into the device illustration. Native pixel aspect ratio is preserved. The complete device scales down to fit the available preview area; drawing does not alter theme fonts or firmware resources.

Validation: compiled for arm64 macOS 14+, visually inspected native previews for the Gen 1/2 ring, Gen 3, Gen 4, Mini 1 and Mini 2; verified wheel controls update menu scrolling and switch to Now Playing. Full-device layout and Gen 3 touch controls were inspected after the final sizing/compositing change. The final packaged engine passed all 20 exports and 80 LCD previews, with all theme payload hashes matching the established baselines. Deep/strict code-signature verification passed. No hardware testing is claimed.
